THE MAGIC
LIVES ON

New Generation Discovers Silent Film Treasures

A peculiar new fashion has swept the picture houses: audiences have begun applauding films that make not a single sound. “Silence still speaks volumes,” declares a most enthusiastic curator.

Witnesses insist a hat blown off in the third reel caused an uproar not seen since the invention of popcorn.

SECTION 02

A BRIGHTER WORLD
ON THE BIG SCREEN

New Theatre Brings People Together Through the Power of Film

The newly restored Rivoli Theatre opened its doors this week, attracting a crowd of astonished patrons who had apparently never considered sitting together in the dark to watch a wall. The proprietor confidently declared it a triumph of science and upholstered seating.

“Movies give us a shared dream space,” said director Clara Bennett at the grand opening. “And a very practical excuse to eat sweets for two consecutive hours.” The house promises astonishing adventures, improbable romances, and a Tuesday matinée devoted entirely to hats.

A peculiar microphone was discovered to have captured an actor's whispered demand for more biscuits. The critics have called the incident “revolutionary.”

SECTION 07

THE GREAT POPCORN QUESTION

Scholars convened yesterday to ask whether a motion picture can truly begin before the last kernel pops. Proceedings were adjourned for refreshments.

SECTION 03

STARS, STORIES
& SECOND ACTS

Beloved Actress Finds New Role Behind the Camera

After decades in the spotlight, the celebrated Clara Vance has reportedly decided to stand on the other side of the camera. Her first instruction was, “Please point it toward the actors.”

The production is a tender account of second chances, enchanted umbrellas, and a missing umbrella stand.
